Kids and families

Updated November 3, 2026

BJJ-US is the app jiu-jitsu gyms use for check-ins, class schedules, belt progress and events. Many gyms teach kids, so this page explains how children use the app, what's in it, and who can see a child's information.

Age rating

The App Store age rating for BJJ-US is 9+. The app is about training and fitness, including optional Apple Health workouts. Its illustrated technique stories show self-defense escapes, such as escaping a headlock.

Parents should still decide what's right for their own child, and stay involved in how the app is used.

Children under 16 use a parent's account

Children under 16 don't have a login of their own. A parent or guardian manages them from the parent's app:

  • Add a child under Profile → Family, with their date of birth. The gym confirms the child and their belt.
  • Switch to the child to check them in to class, sign the gym's waiver for them, and follow their belt progress.
  • Get their notifications, such as a new stripe or belt.
  • Invite the other parent to manage the child too.

From age 16, or earlier if the parent allows it, the parent can give the child their own login with an invite from the app. Until then, the child can't sign in.

What the app includes and doesn't

  • No chat or messaging between members.
  • No public profiles or social feed.
  • No ads.
  • No in-app purchases. Paid gym events, such as seminars, open a secure Stripe checkout page. For a child under 16, a parent signs them up from the parent's account.
  • Some technique lessons link to a video on YouTube or Vimeo, which opens that website.

Content comes from your gym

Technique videos, event flyers and photos are added by your gym's staff, not by BJJ-US, and BJJ-US doesn't review them before they appear. Our Terms of Use don't allow unlawful, harassing or hateful content. If you see something that isn't appropriate, tell your gym, or email support@bjj-us.com.

A child's information

  • A child's personal details, such as their date of birth, emergency contact and any notes for their professor, are visible only to their parents and the gym's professors and admins.
  • Other members may see a child's name and belt, for example in class or event lists. Other gyms can't see it.
  • Arrival check-in is optional and works on the phone of the person who is signed in. If a parent turns it on, arriving at the gym also tells the gym that the kids they checked in made it to class: a yes or no, never a location.
  • Apple Health is optional and saves only the signed-in person's own classes, never a child's.
  • We don't knowingly collect information from a child under 13 without a parent's or guardian's involvement. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

Our App Privacy Notice explains in full what the app collects, who can see it and how to delete it.

Questions

About your child's classes, belt or membership, ask your gym. About the app, email support@bjj-us.com.
