App Privacy Notice

Effective October 30, 2026

BJJ-US is an app that jiu-jitsu gyms use to run their gym: check-ins, the class schedule, belt progress, events and memberships. This notice is for the people who use the app (members, parents, professors and gym staff). It explains what the BJJ-US app and service ("BJJ-US", "we") collect, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it.

BJJ-US is currently offered to gyms in the United States. Your information is stored and processed in the United States.

The short version: we collect what your gym needs to run classes and track your training. Your gym's staff can see your information; other gyms can't. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. Your location never leaves your phone. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

Your gym and us

You join BJJ-US through a gym, using an invite code from that gym. The gym decides what it uses the app for and manages its members' records, so for most of your information the gym is responsible for it and we process it on the gym's behalf. Your gym may also have its own privacy policy, which you'll find in the app under Profile → Privacy & legal; for questions about how your gym uses your information, ask your gym directly. How BJJ-US works with gyms is described in our Privacy Policy for gyms.

What we collect

  • Account: your name, email address and password (stored securely by our login provider; we never see it in plain text), and your preferred language.
  • Membership details: your role at the gym, belt and stripes, promotion history, membership plan and status, and family links if a parent or guardian manages your account.
  • Profile details you or your gym add: date of birth, phone number, an emergency contact, gender (optional, used only for women-only classes) and optional notes for your professor, such as an injury.
  • Training activity: your check-ins, event and competition sign-ups, and competition results.
  • Waivers: the waiver you sign in the app, when you signed it, and your health declaration.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device and your notification settings.
  • Purchases: if your gym sells products or event entries in the app, payment is handled by Stripe. We receive the order details, not your card number.
  • Content staff upload: lesson videos, event photos and similar content posted by your gym's professors and admins.

Health information

Notes for your professor, such as an injury, are optional: you never have to add them to use the app. If your gym uses an in-app waiver, confirming its health declaration is part of signing it. This information is visible only to you, a parent or guardian who manages your account, and your gym's professors and admins. It's used only so your gym can train you safely; it's never used for marketing or shared with anyone else. You can change or remove your notes at any time, and they're deleted when you delete your account.

Location

If you allow it, BJJ-US uses your phone's location to notice when you arrive at your gym and suggest checking in to class. That comparison happens on your phone: your location is never sent to us or to your gym, and we don't keep a location history. You can turn location off in your phone's settings at any time; everything else keeps working. Gym admins can save the gym's own address location so the app knows where the gym is.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your personal information.
  • We don't show ads, and we don't use advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

How we use it

We use your information only to provide the service: letting you check in and see the schedule, showing your progress, letting your gym's staff manage classes, memberships, promotions and events, sending notifications you've chosen to receive, processing purchases, and keeping the service secure and working.

Who can see it

  • You can see your own information, and a parent or guardian can see the accounts of the children they manage.
  • Your gym's professors and admins can see members' information, including profile details, check-ins and waivers, so they can run the gym.
  • Other members may see limited information such as your name and belt, for example in class or event lists.
  • Other gyms can't see your information. Each gym's data is kept separate.

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of our users or the service.

Children

Children's accounts are created and managed by their parent or guardian through their gym. A child can only sign in with their own login if their parent or guardian allows it. We don't knowingly collect information from a child under 13 without a parent's or guardian's involvement. If you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Privacy & legal → Delete my account.

When you delete your account, we delete your login, your name, your profile and contact details, health notes, signed waivers, push tokens, notification settings and family links. Your gym keeps an anonymous record of past check-ins, promotions, competition results, event sign-ups and memberships, labeled "Former member", so its history and books stay correct. Children you manage who have no login of their own and no other guardian are anonymized the same way.

If you're your gym's only admin, you'll be asked to make someone else an admin first, so the gym isn't locked out.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • See your information and get a copy of it;
  • Correct it if it's wrong or out of date;
  • Delete it, along with your account;
  • Withdraw permissions, such as location or notifications, at any time in your phone's settings;
  • Not be treated differently for using any of these rights.

You can view and update most of your information in the app, and delete your account there yourself. For anything else, email support@bjj-us.com or ask your gym. We'll reply within 30 days, and we may need to confirm it's really you first. A parent or guardian can make these requests for a child they manage.

Do Not Track

BJJ-US doesn't track you across other apps or websites, so there is nothing for a browser's "Do Not Track" signal to turn off; we treat every user as if it were on.

This website

bjj-us.com doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies. Our web host, GitHub, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and each gym's data is isolated from every other gym's. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and limit who can access it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the changes are significant, we'll let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@bjj-us.com
