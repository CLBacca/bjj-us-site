Gym Privacy Policy Template

Template version November 2, 2026

Your members should know how your gym uses their information. This template describes what BJJ-US does, so you can adapt it and post it on your gym's website. Then add the link in the app under Profile → Gym details, along with your gym's email and phone. Your members will find all three under Profile → Privacy & legal.

How to use it

  • Replace everything in [brackets] with your gym's details.
  • Keep the optional sections that apply to your gym and delete the rest.
  • Add anything else your gym does with personal information that isn't in the app, such as paper forms, security cameras or a separate billing system.

This is a starting point, not legal advice. Your gym is responsible for its own privacy policy. Laws vary by state, so have it reviewed by a lawyer, especially if you train children or collect health information.

[Gym Name] Privacy Policy

Effective [date]

[Gym legal name] ("[Gym Name]", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect about our members, students, parents and visitors, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information when you join, train, sign up for events, or use our gym app, BJJ-US:

  • Contact and account details: your name, email address, phone number and the login you create for the app.
  • Membership details: your membership plan and status, belt and stripes, promotion history, and family links if a parent or guardian manages your account.
  • Personal details: date of birth, an emergency contact, and optionally your gender (used only for women-only classes).
  • Health information: optional notes you share with your instructors, such as an injury, and the health declaration in our waiver.
  • Waivers: the waiver you sign, online or in the app, when you signed it, and your signature.
  • Training activity: class check-ins, and event and competition sign-ups and results.
  • Purchases: what you buy from us. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we don't see or store your card number.
  • [Optional: Trial classes: if you ask about classes or book a trial, your name, contact details and how you heard about us.]

How we use it

  • To run classes, check you in and track your progress and promotions.
  • To manage memberships, waivers, events and competitions.
  • To keep you safe on the mat, and to contact your emergency contact if needed.
  • To send you class updates and gym news through the app, if you turn on notifications.
  • To process payments and keep our records.
  • [Optional: To send you our newsletter or offers by email. You can unsubscribe at any time.]

Health information

Sharing health notes is optional. Only you, a parent or guardian who manages your account, and our instructors and staff can see them, and we use them only to train you safely. We never use health information for marketing.

Our gym app

We use the BJJ-US app to run our gym. BJJ-US stores and processes our members' information on our behalf, only to provide the app to us, and doesn't sell it or use it for advertising. Your phone's location, if you allow it, is used only on your phone to remind you to check in when you arrive; it isn't sent to us or to BJJ-US. You can read more in the BJJ-US App Privacy Notice.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the gym, such as BJJ-US and our payment processor, under their own privacy commitments;
  • with competition organizers, when you ask us to register you for an event;
  • when required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

Other members can see limited information, such as your name and belt, in class and event lists in the app.

Photos and video

[Optional: We sometimes take photos and videos in class and at events and may post them on our website or social media. If you'd prefer not to appear, tell an instructor or email us, and we'll respect your choice.]

Children

For students under 18, a parent or guardian provides their information and manages their account. A child can only use the app with their own login if their parent or guardian allows it. Parents and guardians can review, update or delete their child's information at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're a member. After you leave, we keep [membership, waiver and payment records for (number) years] for our records, legal and insurance purposes, then delete it. If you delete your app account, your personal details are removed, and we keep an anonymous record of your past attendance and promotions.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and update most of your information in the app.
  • You can delete your app account at any time in the app: Profile → Privacy & legal → Delete my account.
  • You can turn off notifications or location in your phone's settings at any time.
  • To get a copy of your information, correct it, or ask us to delete it, contact us below. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and let members know.
