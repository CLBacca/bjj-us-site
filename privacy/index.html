Privacy Policy for Gyms

Effective October 30, 2026

This policy is for the gyms that use BJJ-US ("you", "your gym"). It explains how BJJ-US ("we") handles the information your gym and your members put into the app, what we promise to do with it, and what we rely on your gym to do.

Using the app as a member? Read the App Privacy Notice instead. Need a privacy policy for your gym's website? Start from our gym privacy policy template.

BJJ-US is currently offered to gyms in the United States. All data is stored and processed in the United States.

Who is responsible for what

  • Your gym owns its members' data. Your gym decides who to invite, what to record about members, which features to use and how long to keep records. For this data, your gym is the business responsible for it, and BJJ-US is a service provider that stores and processes it for you.
  • BJJ-US is responsible for its own data: the contact and billing details of your gym's account with us, our support conversations with you, and this website.

What we do with your gym's data

We use your gym's data only to provide BJJ-US to your gym and its members: running the app, sending the notifications your gym and members choose, processing purchases, providing support you ask for, and keeping the service secure. We do not:

  • sell or rent your gym's or your members' data;
  • use it for advertising, or market to your members;
  • share it with other gyms, or combine it with other gyms' data;
  • contact your members, except for messages the app sends as part of the service (such as sign-up and password emails, and the notifications your gym and members turn on).

We may disclose data if required by law. Where the law allows, we'll tell your gym first.

What the app processes for your gym

Depending on the features your gym uses: members' names, contact details and logins; belts, stripes and promotion history; membership plans and status; check-ins; date of birth, emergency contacts, optional gender and health notes; signed waivers; family links for parents and kids; event and competition sign-ups and results; leads and trial classes; content your staff upload, such as lesson videos and event photos; and push notification tokens. Members' phone location is never sent to us. The App Privacy Notice describes all of this to your members.

Keeping gyms separate

Every gym's data is isolated from every other gym's at the database level, not just in the app's screens: a member, professor or admin of one gym can't read or change another gym's data. Inside your gym, what people can see depends on their role (student, professor or admin), which your gym's admins control.

Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit between the app, our servers and our providers.
  • Passwords are handled by our login provider and are never visible to us or to your gym.
  • Member photos, videos and other files are served through links that expire.
  • Access to production systems is limited to the people who run BJJ-US, and we access your gym's data only to provide the service, give support you request, fix problems, or when the law requires it.

No system is perfectly secure. If we learn of a security incident that affects your gym's data, we'll notify your gym's admins without undue delay, tell you what we know, and help you meet any obligation you have to notify your members.

Service providers

We use these providers to run BJJ-US. Each processes data only to provide its service to us:

  • Supabase: login, database and file storage
  • Render: hosting for our servers
  • Stripe: payments
  • Expo, with Apple and Google: delivering push notifications

We'll update this list before adding a provider that processes members' personal data.

Member requests

Members can view and update most of their information in the app, and delete their own account there at any time. When a member asks your gym for a copy of their data, a correction or deletion that the app doesn't cover, contact us at support@bjj-us.com and we'll help you respond. If a member contacts us directly about data your gym controls, we'll refer them to your gym or handle it with your gym's knowledge.

When a member deletes their account, their personal details are deleted, and your gym keeps an anonymous record of their past check-ins, promotions, results and memberships, so your history and books stay correct.

If your gym stops using BJJ-US

Your gym can ask for an export of its data, such as its member list and attendance history, for 30 days after its account ends. After that, we delete your gym's data within 90 days, except where the law requires us to keep something, such as billing records.

What we rely on your gym to do

  • Tell your members how your gym uses their information, for example with your own privacy policy. Our template is a starting point.
  • Only invite people who have agreed to join, and only record information you have a reason to keep.
  • Children: add a child only with their parent's or guardian's permission, and give a child their own login only when their parent or guardian agrees.
  • Health information: treat health notes and waiver declarations as confidential, and keep them to what's needed to train people safely.
  • Waivers: your gym is responsible for the content of its waiver; have it reviewed for your state.
  • Staff access: give professor and admin roles only to people who need them, and remove access when someone leaves.
  • Content: upload only videos, photos and other content your gym has the right to use, and get permission before posting photos of members.

Information about your gym's account

For your gym's account with us, we keep your gym's name, branding and location, and the names, emails and billing details of your owners and admins. We use them to provide the service, bill your subscription, and contact you about your account and important changes. We don't sell this information.

This website

bjj-us.com doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads nothing from other companies. Our web host, GitHub, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. If the changes are significant, we'll tell your gym's admins before they take effect.
