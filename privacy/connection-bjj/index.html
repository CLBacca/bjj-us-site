Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Connection BJJ Academy ("Connection BJJ", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect about our members, students, parents and visitors, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect information when you try a class, join, train, sign up for events, or use our gym app, BJJ-US:

  • Contact and account details: your name, email address, phone number and the login you create for the app.
  • Membership details: your membership plan and status, belt and stripes, promotion history, and family links if a parent or guardian manages your account.
  • Personal details: date of birth, an emergency contact, and optionally your gender (used only for women-only classes).
  • Health information: optional notes you share with your instructors, such as an injury, and the health declaration in our waiver.
  • Waivers: the waiver you sign, online or in the app, when you signed it, and your signature. When you sign online we also record your device and internet address as proof of signing.
  • Training activity: class check-ins, and event and competition sign-ups and results.
  • Purchases and billing: your membership payments and what you buy from us. Card payments are handled by our payment processor; we don't see or store your full card number.
  • Trial classes: if you ask about classes or book a trial, your name, contact details and how you heard about us.

How we use it

  • To run classes, check you in and track your progress and promotions.
  • To manage memberships, billing, waivers, events and competitions.
  • To keep you safe on the mat, and to contact your emergency contact if needed.
  • To send you class updates and gym news through the app, if you turn on notifications, and in our WhatsApp groups, if you join them.
  • To follow up after a trial class.
  • To process payments and keep our records.

Health information

Sharing health notes is optional. Only you, a parent or guardian who manages your account, and our instructors and staff can see them, and we use them only to train you safely. We never use health information for marketing.

Our gym app and other services

We use the BJJ-US app to run our gym. BJJ-US stores and processes our members' information on our behalf, only to provide the app to us, and doesn't sell it or use it for advertising. Your phone's location, if you allow it, is used only on your phone to remind you to check in when you arrive, and to let us know you made it to a class you checked in to — we learn only that you made it, never where you were or when you arrived. If your phone didn't see you arrive, the app asks you instead, and after the class an instructor confirms anyone who didn't answer. Your location itself isn't sent to us or to BJJ-US. You can read more in the BJJ-US App Privacy Notice.

We also use Kicksite for membership and billing records. Kicksite stores that information on our behalf, under its own privacy commitments.

WhatsApp groups

We share class updates and gym news in WhatsApp groups. Joining is optional, and you can leave at any time. Other members of a group can see your name, phone number and messages. WhatsApp is run by Meta under its own privacy policy, and we don't control how it handles your information.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with service providers that help us run the gym, such as BJJ-US, Kicksite and our payment processor, under their own privacy commitments;
  • with competition organizers, when you ask us to register you for an event;
  • when required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

Other members can see limited information, such as your name and belt, in class and event lists in the app.

Photos and video

We sometimes take photos and videos in class and at events and may post them on our website or social media. If you'd prefer not to appear, tell an instructor or email us, and we'll respect your choice.

Children

For students under 18, a parent or guardian provides their information, signs the waiver and manages their account. A child can only use the app with their own login if their parent or guardian allows it. Parents and guardians can review, update or delete their child's information at any time.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you're a member. After you leave, we keep membership, waiver and payment records for 7 years for our records, legal and insurance purposes, then delete them. If you delete your app account, your personal details are removed, and we keep an anonymous record of your past attendance and promotions.

Your choices and rights

  • You can see and update most of your information in the app.
  • You can delete your app account at any time in the app: Profile → Privacy & legal → Delete my account.
  • You can turn off notifications or location in your phone's settings at any time.
  • To get a copy of your information, correct it, or ask us to delete it, contact us below. We'll reply within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and let members know.
