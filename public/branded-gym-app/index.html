Your brand

A branded app for your BJJ gym

With BJJ-US, members open an app with your gym's name, logo and colors, and you never build or publish an app yourself. Members download BJJ-US, join with your gym's invite code, and from then on the app shows your gym.

What members see

  • Your gym's name, logo and colors throughout the app.
  • Your class schedule, with what's on now and next.
  • Your lesson library and the week's focus, from your professors.
  • Your events: seminars, camps and competitions, with sign-up in the app.
  • Your waiver, and an online waiver page for trial students in your logo and color.

Change it anytime

Change your logo or colors and every member sees the new look right away. There's no app update to wait for and no extra fee.

Why one app instead of your own

Publishing your own app means a developer account, app store reviews and updates to maintain. With BJJ-US there's one app in the App Store, and every gym gets new features as soon as they ship. The app's icon and store name are BJJ-US; everything inside is your gym.

Your gym's data stays yours

Each gym's members, attendance and payments are kept separate from every other gym's.

English and Portuguese

Each member can use the app in English or Portuguese, and notifications follow their choice.

Questions

Will my gym have its own listing in the App Store?

No. Members download BJJ-US from the App Store. Once they join with your gym's invite code, the app shows your gym's name, logo and colors.

Does branding cost extra?

No. Your name, logo and colors are included in every tier, starting with a 14-day free trial.

How do members join my gym in the app?

With an invite code from your gym. You can upload your member list as a spreadsheet (CSV) on the BJJ-US admin website, and BJJ-US creates an invite code for each member.

Is the app available in Portuguese?

Yes. Each member can switch the app to Portuguese in their profile.
