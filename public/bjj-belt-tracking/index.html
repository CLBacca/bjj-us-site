Belts and promotions

BJJ belt and stripe tracking

BJJ-US keeps every member's belt, stripes and promotions, for the adult and kids belt systems. It counts time at belt and training days, and shows professors who meets your gym's guidelines for the next stripe or belt. The professor always decides.

Adult and kids belts, by age

BJJ-US follows the IBJJF belt system and offers the right belts for each member's age.

  • Kids, through age 15: white, then gray, yellow, orange and green belts, each in white, solid and black versions.
  • Adults, from 16: white, blue, purple, brown and black, then coral and red belts. Belts start at the IBJJF minimum ages: blue and purple at 16, brown at 18 and black at 19.
  • Stripes and degrees: up to 4 stripes on every belt below black, and up to 6 degrees on a black belt.

Who's ready for the next stripe or belt

BJJ-US counts each member's training days from their attendance, one per day, and their months at their current belt. Then it compares them with your gym's guidelines.

  • Promotions due lists the members who meet the guidelines for their next rank.
  • Stripe countdowns show how many training days each member has left until their next stripe.
  • Members see their own progress: belt and stripes, classes trained, streaks and badges, promotions and competition results.

BJJ-US never promotes anyone by itself. A professor awards every stripe and belt, and the member gets a notification.

Questions

Does BJJ-US track kids belts?

Yes. Members through age 15 use the kids belt system: white, then gray, yellow, orange and green, each in white, solid and black versions, with up to 4 stripes. From 16, members use the adult belts.

Can my gym set its own promotion requirements?

Yes. Admins set training days per stripe, training days for the belt and months at the belt. Blue, purple and brown belts never go below the IBJJF minimum time.

Does the app promote students automatically?

No. It shows who meets your gym's guidelines. A professor awards every stripe and belt.

How long does it take to get a blue belt in BJJ?

IBJJF sets no minimum time at white belt, so each gym decides. BJJ-US recommends 12 months and 125 training days at white belt, and your gym can change both. The IBJJF minimum age for a blue belt is 16.
