Attendance

The BJJ attendance app with confirmed check-ins

BJJ-US is an attendance app for Brazilian jiu-jitsu gyms. Members check in to class from their phone, and every check-in is confirmed. Your attendance shows who actually trained, and it feeds belt progress, promotions and reports.

How every check-in is confirmed

A check-in is confirmed in one of three ways, in this order:

  • The phone confirms arrival. With arrival check-in on, the member's phone tells the gym "made it" when it reaches the gym: yes or no, nothing else.
  • The member answers. Otherwise the app asks "Did you make it?" Members can answer until 15 minutes after class, and parents answer for the kids they checked in.
  • The professor approves. After class, professors get a reminder to approve or remove whoever is left, one by one or all at once.

Checking in to class

  • Members check in from the class schedule, up to 36 hours ahead.
  • The app can remind them before class, and again when they arrive at the gym.
  • Parents check in their kids from their own phone.
  • Professors can add or remove check-ins for the last 7 days, so a forgotten check-in is easy to fix.

What attendance powers

  • Belt progress. Each day a member trains counts toward their next stripe and belt, by your gym's promotion guidelines. See belt and stripe tracking.
  • Class attendance. Professors see who made it to each class, who was approved and who isn't confirmed yet.
  • Reports. Owners see attendance and membership reports.
  • Apple Health. On iPhone, members can save each confirmed class as a Martial Arts workout, with estimated calories. It's optional.

Locations stay on the phone

Arrival check-in is optional. When it's on, the gym learns only whether a member made it to a class they checked in to. Members' locations never leave their phones, and they can turn arrival check-in off at any time; everything else keeps working.

Questions

How does BJJ-US confirm attendance?

The member's phone confirms they arrived at the gym, or the member answers "Did you make it?", or a professor approves the check-in after class.

Does the app track where members are?

No. Arrival check-in is optional, and the gym learns only whether a member made it to a class they checked in to. The location never leaves the phone.

What if someone forgets to check in?

A professor can add or remove check-ins for the last 7 days.

Can parents check in their kids?

Yes. Parents check in their children from their own app and answer "Did you make it?" for them.
