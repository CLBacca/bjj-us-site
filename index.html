One app, three views

Built for everyone on the mat

Members, professors and owners each see what they need. Every gym gets its own branding, and its data stays its own.

Members

Train and track

  • Check in to class in one tap
  • The weekly schedule, with what's on now and next
  • Belt, stripes and training days at a glance
  • Events, competitions and the gym's lesson library
  • Parents manage their kids from one login

Professors

Run the class

  • See who's on the mat today
  • Add or fix check-ins for the past week
  • Award stripes and belts
  • Post the weekly focus and lesson videos
  • A round timer built in

Owners

Grow the gym

  • Attendance and membership reports
  • Membership plans and member status
  • Leads and trial classes
  • Waivers signed in the app
  • Change your logo and colors anytime, live for everyone

Getting started

We create your gym and send the owner an admin invite code.

Members get their codes, download BJJ-US and join your gym.
