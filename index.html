Attendance

Attendance you can trust

Every check-in is confirmed, in this order. Your members' locations never leave their phones.

The phone arrives

With arrival check-in on, the member's phone tells the gym "made it": yes or no, nothing else.

The member answers

Otherwise the app asks "Did you make it?" Parents answer for their kids.

The professor approves

After class, a reminder to approve or remove whoever is left.

Optional on iPhone: each confirmed class is saved to Apple Health as a Martial Arts workout, with estimated calories.
